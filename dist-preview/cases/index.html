ReCover

Circular design and business model for medical face masks.

Client
DSM N.V., with iLINQ and Dijkgraaf Strategisch Advies
Challenge
In 2020 the region depended on overseas suppliers for protective equipment, and locally made masks were designed for incineration, destroying large volumes of high-value, non-renewable materials. Speed had come before sustainability.
Approach
Together with Dutch PPE Solutions (a joint venture of VDL and DSM), Maasstad hospital, Renewi and PreZero we mapped a circular value chain. Interviews, brainstorms and co-creation with hospital staff tested the legal, medical, design and material criteria and defined roles for a pilot.
Result
A complete concept design for the product and value chain, a feasibility assessment with a visual pilot roadmap, tested strategies for design and recycling, and an action plan for commercialisation. The work became the basis for a Dutch government procurement project.

University canteen

A glazed extension for a university canteen, built around a tree that had to stay.

Client
Theologische Universiteit Apeldoorn (TUA)
Challenge
The university canteen needed to grow, but the site left little room: a protected tree could not be touched and the plot was tight. Standard solutions did not fit.
Approach
We looked for what the site did allow and developed three different concepts. The concept we developed furthest is a light glass extension with a roof that spans the tree. Together with Wouter Meijer we presented the concepts to the municipality, supported the first steps towards a permit and presented the plans to the neighbourhood.
Result
A positive reception from the municipality and the neighbours. The university is now raising funds to realise the plan.

Qumey

Conceptual design for a new circular product range.

Client
Qumey Metaalindustrie B.V.
Challenge
Qumey wanted to show stakeholders and the industry what its new circular product range could be.
Approach
We defined and prioritised the key design criteria with Qumey, made a first digital concept and refined design and story in online feedback sessions.
Result
One conceptual image that shows the different designs, the sustainability story and the unique features, and helps Qumey communicate its product vision.

Interim management

Keeping a soil investigation and archaeology firm running, then making it run better.

Client
Soil investigation and archaeology firm, Amersfoort (ongoing)
Challenge
After seven years the manager left. The organisation had to keep running while nobody yet knew where its processes were fragile.
Approach
Stepped in to keep operations going, then brought structure to the processes. First exposed the bottlenecks, now automating the repetitive work wherever possible.
Result
Continuity through the handover, clearer processes and less manual work, with the next improvements already in progress.

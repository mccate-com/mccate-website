Services

Three short ways to get moving, for organisations in spatial development and innovation. Each one starts with the people who have to decide.

Quick scan

In one to two weeks we map where your project or process stalls and what would unblock it fastest. You get a clear picture on one page and a short list of what to do first.

  • Conversations with the people involved
  • One-page overview of bottlenecks and options
  • Recommended first steps, ranked by impact

Decision sparring

A sounding board for the people who have to decide. We bring an outside view, summarise the big picture and help you weigh the options, with attention to the process and to the people it affects.

  • Preparing and structuring a decision
  • Aligning stakeholders before the meeting
  • A follow-up check after the decision

Improvement sprint

When an improvement needs hands as well as advice, we lead a short sprint with a clear goal and then hand over. This is interim management in its short, focused form.

  • Fixed goal and duration, usually four to twelve weeks
  • Keep operations running, find the bottlenecks, automate what repeats
  • Active in your team, then a clean handover
  • Longer interim assignments on request

Not sure which fits? Describe your situation and we will tell you what we would do first.

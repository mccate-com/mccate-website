About

Human-centred advice for projects that need to move. Founded to optimise performance while creating positive change for every stakeholder.

Friso Kater

Founder

From a young age I have been drawn to exploring new ideas and challenging existing frameworks. I made that curiosity my profession.

I like to make a short, useful contribution: summarising the big picture quickly, spotting what can be improved and helping people take the next decision.

My background lets me guide innovation in a systematic way: identifying customer needs, translating them into creative and strategic solutions, communicating through visual concepts and shaping product and brand propositions.

Today I work as interim manager for a soil investigation and archaeology firm, where spatial development begins. I have (co-)founded companies and built consortia in both the Netherlands and Sweden. That taught me to understand and act on different communication styles, and to turn a strategic plan into execution quickly.
